# cdpfleet residential: one sticky US identity

No proxy of your own: a residential US IP that stays the same for 30 minutes — and across browser sessions that reuse the session name. Tier 1 ($5/GB) from your balance; every account starts with $5 free credit. See [residential proxy](https://cdpfleet.com/docs/proxies#residential).
